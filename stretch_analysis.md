# Stretch Embedding Explorer Analysis

The t-SNE visualization of the GloVe word embeddings revealed clear semantic clustering between categories. Sports-related words such as “football,” “basketball,” and “tennis” formed a dense cluster, while technology words like “computer,” “software,” and “internet” appeared grouped together in another region of the embedding space. Finance and technology showed slight overlap because many financial concepts are related to digital technology and business systems. Emotion-related words were more spread out, suggesting broader semantic usage across contexts. Some selected words were not included because they were out-of-vocabulary in the reduced GloVe dataset.

The DistilBERT document embedding visualization also showed meaningful structure among the BBC News articles. Sport articles formed one of the clearest clusters, indicating strong topical similarity. Business and politics articles partially overlapped because economic and governmental topics are often related in news reporting. Technology articles appeared closer to business than entertainment, reflecting the semantic relationships learned by DistilBERT. Overall, the visualizations demonstrated that both word-level and document-level embeddings successfully captured semantic meaning and organized related concepts near each other in vector space.
